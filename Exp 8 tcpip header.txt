Step 2: Start the capture

Open Wireshark.
On the home screen, double-click your active interface, which is the one with a moving graph line next to it (usually Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
Capture starts immediately.

Step 3: Generate TCP traffic

While capturing, open CMD and run:
curl http://example.com
curl -I https://www.google.com
ping google.com

Step 4: Stop the capture

Click the red square (Stop) button in the toolbar.

Step 5: Filter TCP packets

In the display filter bar at the top, type tcp and press Enter.

Useful extra filters:(type individually)

tcp.flags.syn == 1
ip.addr == 93.184.216.34
tcp.port == 80
